Photo-backed sign-in, mobile pass, and a date picker on the availability board

Sign-in hero
- The green panel is now the Kulliyyah photograph behind a gradient scrim,
  layered over solid green as the fallback if the image is missing.
- The scrim never drops below .74 opacity. At that strength white text clears
  5.3:1 against the worst case, a pure-white photograph, so the picture can be
  swapped without re-checking contrast. Body copy moved from .86 to .92 white
  for the same reason.
- Swapping the picture is one line: the --login-photo custom property.

Mobile
- Five breakpoints rather than three: 1180, 1024 (tablet, four-across becomes
  two), 940, 640 and 420.
- Touch targets at 44px minimum on buttons, inputs, segmented controls,
  pagination and the collapsed navigation. Inputs go to 16px on phones, which
  is what stops iOS zooming the page on focus.
- Wide tables and the availability board scroll inside their own container;
  the page itself never scrolls sideways. The board keeps a 660px minimum so
  seven days stay legible instead of being crushed.
- The day picker scrolls horizontally rather than stacking into a tall block;
  modals dock to the bottom of the viewport; button groups go full width.

Availability board
- The seven-day button strip is replaced by a date picker that spells out the
  weekday beside it, with Today and Tomorrow called out. A date alone does not
  tell you it is a Friday, which is usually what decides whether a slot is any
  use.
- Bounded to the 90-day advance limit, and a date typed outside the window
  clamps to the nearest end rather than blanking the board.
- The cars view still shows a week, taking the chosen date as its first day,
  because a trip runs across days.
