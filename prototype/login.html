A single place for AIKOL students, lecturers and staff to check venue availability, submit booking requests for venues and Kulliyyah vehicles, and follow their status.

  • Booking requests submitted online, without paper forms
  • Automatic prevention of double bookings
  • Complete booking history for reference and audit
