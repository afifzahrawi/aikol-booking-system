Room and Vehicle Booking System

This prototype demonstrates the proposed user interface of the AIKOL Room and Vehicle Booking System. It runs entirely in the browser with fictional demonstration data and is intended to support requirement confirmation before development begins. It reflects the decisions AIKOL confirmed in section 24 of the management report, together with the follow-up decisions on vehicles, self-registration, confirmation email and administrator-managed facilities.

Demonstration prototype — no live data All venues, vehicles, users, bookings and images shown are fictional and were created for design review only. They do not represent actual AIKOL facilities, personnel or booking records. Demonstration accounts use @demo.aikol.test addresses so they are obviously fictional; the live system requires an @iium.edu.my or @live.iium.edu.my address. All photographs are generated placeholders and must be replaced with real ones supplied by the Kulliyyah.

About this design

These screens implement the AIKOL design supplied by the Kulliyyah (design/AIKOL Booking 1a.dc.html):

  • Green #14675b and gold #d99b28 on warm paper, with the real AIKOL wordmark.
  • Amiri for headings and resource names, IBM Plex Sans for everything else.
  • A pointed-arch frame on the building photograph and room galleries — the identity is carried by the arch and the Arabic greeting, not applied ornament.
  • Horizontal green navigation replaces the old left sidebar.
  • A new availability board: rooms by the hour, cars by the day, with free time clickable straight into a request.

The business rules are unchanged and still confirmed: every booking is approved, 08:00–22:00, 9 hours maximum, 90 days ahead, cancellation up to 3 days before with a reason.
