Email delivery

Django sends queued account and booking messages through the SMTP server entered here.

Ask the administrator of your sending mailbox for its SMTP host, port, sign-in method and permitted From address. This form supports SMTP username and password; it does not support OAuth-only mailboxes. Do not paste credentials into a support chat.

Enabling delivery will also send messages already waiting in the outbox on the next scheduled run. Review the queued count above first.
